# Opportunity Filter

A dead-simple **YES / NO** gut-check for deciding whether a new opportunity is worth your time — built to stop you from overcommitting.

## How to use

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no server, no account. Your answers stay in the browser.

1. Type what the opportunity is.
2. Answer 6 quick yes/no questions.
3. Get a clear verdict.

## The logic

It's built on the **"hell yeah or no"** rule: if it isn't a clear yes, it's a no.

- **Dealbreakers** (do I have the time? is it bounded?) can single-handedly force a **NO**, no matter how exciting the rest is. This is what protects you from overcommitting.
- The remaining "value" questions (does it move a real goal, will I be glad I said yes, am I well-suited, can I exit) need to land strongly. Pass all (or all but one) → **YES**. Lukewarm → **NO**.

The point isn't to find reasons to say yes — it's to make a fast, honest call and keep your calendar open for the things that are a true *hell yeah*.

Use **Copy decision** to paste the result into your notes, calendar invite, or a reply.
