# Newborn Timeline 👶

An interactive, single-file timeline visualization of a newborn's first three months —
**what to expect** at each stage and **how to deal with it**, plus per-stage "when to call
your pediatrician" red flags.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies, fully offline.

- Tap any dot on the timeline (or use the **←/→** arrow keys) to move through time.
- The pulsing **"you are here"** marker is set to **11 days old** (week 2). Edit the
  `here: true` flag and the age badge text in `index.html` to re-center it as baby grows.

## Stages covered

Days 0–7 · Days 8–14 · Weeks 3–4 · Weeks 5–6 · Weeks 7–9 · Weeks 10–12

## Disclaimer

General information for tired parents, **not medical advice**. Every baby develops on their
own schedule. For anything that worries you, contact your pediatrician or midwife.
